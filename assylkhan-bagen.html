Assylkhan Bagen | Асылхан Баген

Photographer · Photojournalist · Author · Cultural Documentarian

Assylkhan Bagen (born 7 November 1941 in Xinjiang, China) is a Chinese Kazakh photographer, photojournalist, editor and author whose work documents Kazakh traditions, everyday life and cultural heritage. His career included editorial and photographic work for Chinese pictorial publications. Steppe Legacy is the official family website and cultural archive dedicated to preserving and presenting his photographic legacy and published works.

Photographic albums

  • The Yurt Culture (2001)
  • Kazakh's Custom and Culture (2004)
  • Kazakh Nomadic Culture (2014), also titled Қазақтың көшпелілік мәдениеті

He also authored the literary work Қасен генерал (1992). The list above highlights three photographic albums documented by the family and is not a complete bibliography.

Library Archive & High-Resolution Collector’s Edition

A digitized copy of Kazakh Nomadic Culture is available through the Kazakhstan National Electronic Library (KazNEB).

Unlike the library’s scanned copy, the Steppe Legacy Digital Collector’s Edition is produced directly from the original high-resolution files, offering greater clarity and a richer viewing experience of Assylkhan Bagen’s photography.
